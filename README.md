## Featured project

### [Malaysia Grocery Price Analysis](https://github.com/qylla/malaysia-grocery-price-analysis)
Analysis of 517,865 KPDN PriceCatcher records (Jul 2025 – Jun 2026) for chicken, eggs and cooking oil.
- Chicken in Sabah costs over 50% more than in Johor, and the East–West gap holds within every store type.
- Found four sudden monthly price increases (5%+) and confirmed with a same-store test that they are real price moves, not a reporting artefact.
- Cleaned the data using price ranges chosen from each item's distribution, removing 0.03% of rows.
